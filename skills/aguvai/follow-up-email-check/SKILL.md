# Follow-up email check

Read the draft follow-up email the user gives you. Check it against these five basics, then reply with a short list: what passes, what to fix, and a tightened version.

1. **One clear next step.** The email asks for exactly one thing, with a date or time.
2. **Their words, not ours.** It repeats one specific thing the buyer said on the call.
3. **Short.** Under 120 words. Cut greetings and filler.
4. **No new claims.** Every fact in it was said on the call or is in the notes the user shared. Flag anything you cannot trace.
5. **Subject line.** Under 8 words and names the next step.

Do not send the email. Do not add facts that are not in what the user gave you.
